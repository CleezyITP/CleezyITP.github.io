Helping people act on personalized offers

My synthesis of Verizon's offer research shaped a tile redesign, and the design changes contributed to a 25% increase in offer acceptance.

Org · and Year
Verizon, 2025
Role · and Team
Lead managing two junior researchers
Methods
Analytics, interviews, and usability testing
Partners
Marketing, product strategy and experience management

Why were customers bouncing off offers that were built for them?

Context

Verizon was introducing multi-offer tiles to bring personalized offers together across the My Verizon app and website, and offer engagement had declined.

Approach

I led the research design and delivery over multiple rounds, managing two researchers: one focused on accessibility and one on mixed methods. We paired behavioral telemetry with in-depth interviews, and I synthesized findings across several offer studies, including a color study and tile usability testing. The data showed where people dropped off. The interviews explained why: problems with visual hierarchy, color and typography, including brand color combinations that added friction.

The synthesis became a set of experience principles for offer strategy, including communicating value clearly and engaging people through deep, data-driven personalization. I shared them with marketing, product strategy and experience management, and design used them to rework tile layout, color and placement.

What changed

Those design changes contributed to a 25% increase in offer acceptance.
